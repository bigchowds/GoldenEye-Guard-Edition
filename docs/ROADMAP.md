# Future map ideas

These are recorded design ideas, **not implemented features or release promises**. This beta remains Runway-only.

## First candidates

1. **Streets — Grunt/Agent.** AI Bond follows the escape route while the player possesses existing guards. Preserve the pursuit/time-limit behavior and civilian objective. Investigate whether he should choose the tank or complete the route on foot. Reuse the now-tested hostile-tank driver, targeting and run-over handling where appropriate.
2. **Dam — Secret Grunt/Secret Agent.** AI Bond destroys the required alarms, reaches the platform and performs the bungee escape. Evaluate the gate/lock interaction and native route before adding shortcuts. If the lock is the only blocker, an explicitly documented open-gate prototype is an option.

These are the user's preferred next maps. No map work is included in this update.

## Later: Facility — Grunt/Agent

Proposed flow: gain entry to the lab, rendezvous with Trevelyan/006, place mines behind the first tanks in the bottling room, and detonate at the escape encounter. Keep scientist casualties under the mission limit.

Investigate the native Ourumov arrival/006 sequence. If that interaction is too fragile for the prototype, a proposed fallback is to protect Trevelyan from damage, have Bond finish placing the mines and move to the middle of the room, then detonate after a five-second pause. This is a future design option; it is not active in this beta.

## Longer term

Boss possession ideas include Jaws in Aztec and Ourumov on Train. Stage-specific guard health, weapons, objectives, navigation and failure conditions need separate design and testing.
